The Name

Why Velvet Volute?

Like a volute, a great fragrance doesn't sit still; it drifts, curls, dances and lingers long after you've passed by. Velvet is for the feel of it: smooth, rich and a little indulgent.

Velvet Volute exists so you can try those fragrances without committing to a full bottle. Every decant is poured by hand from an authentic bottle, so you get the real thing, just in a size that lets you find what you love.

You've smelled something on someone, looked it up, and found out the bottle is $400. That's where a decant comes in. Here's what one is, how I pour them, what they cost, and how to get one in your hands in Ottawa.

## What a decant is

A decant is a small amount of fragrance transferred from a full bottle into a new, smaller vial. Same juice, smaller size. A 2 ml vial is about 25 sprays. A 10 ml is about 120. Enough to live with a scent for a few weeks before you decide whether it deserves a full bottle.

It's not a knock-off, a clone or a "dupe". It's the real fragrance in a size that lets you try it properly.

## How Velvet Volute pours

Every decant here is hand-poured from a sealed full bottle that I bought and opened myself. The bottle gets decanted into a fresh glass vial, labelled with the name and size, capped and packed. Nothing is pre-filled months ahead. Your vial is poured when you order it.

Three vial styles:

- **Classic** · 2, 5 or 10 ml. A clear glass vial with a fine-mist atomizer. The everyday decant.
- **Luxury** · 3, 5 or 10 ml. A weighted glass bottle with a gold cap. The gift-ready one.
- **Roll-on** · 5 or 10 ml. Glass, no spray. For the wrist and neck, travel and the office.

## How to choose

Start small. A 2 ml vial worn three times in the setting you'd actually wear it tells you more than any review. Then:

1. **Pick a family you already like.** Sweet, fresh, spicy, woody, leather or floral. If you love [Jazz Club](/fragrance/maison-margiela-replica-jazz-club/), its cousins in the Leather and Sweet families are the safest next step.
2. **Match the season.** Warm, sweet and leather scents bloom in the cold. Fresh, citrus and tea scents are built for summer and the office.
3. **Ask the Scent Sherpa.** The [quiz on the homepage](/#quiz) asks who's wearing it and what you like, then explains why each pick fits.
4. **Read Fragrance 101.** [EDT, EDP, Extrait and Parfum](/fragrance-101) explained, plus a beginner's crash course.

If you're new to niche, [Nishane Hacivat](/fragrance/nishane-hacivat/) and [Tempfluo](/fragrance/nishane-tempfluo/) are the two most people fall for first. Both work for him, for her, for anyone.

## FAQ

### Is a decant the real fragrance?

Yes. Every decant here comes from a sealed full bottle that I bought and opened myself, poured into a new vial. Velvet Volute isn't affiliated with any fragrance house, and the original bottle and packaging aren't included.

### How long does a decant last before it goes off?

Stored cool and dark with the cap on, a decant keeps the way the bottle does: years, not weeks. Heat and sunlight are what age a fragrance. Don't keep it in the bathroom or the car.

### Can I return a decant if I don't like it?

No. Every vial is poured to order and can't be resold once it leaves here, which is standard for Canadian decant shops. That's exactly what the 2 ml size is for.

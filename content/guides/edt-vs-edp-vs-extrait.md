The words on the bottle aren't decoration. Eau de Toilette, Eau de Parfum, Extrait de Parfum and Parfum tell you how much fragrance oil is in the juice versus alcohol and water. That one number shapes how long a scent lasts, how close it sits to your skin, how many sprays you need, and, if you're buying decants, how long a vial will actually last you.

Here's the short version. The full beginner's course is in [Fragrance 101](/fragrance-101).

## The four tiers

| Label | Oil | Lasts | Sprays per wear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Eau de Toilette (EDT) | about 5 to 15% | roughly 3 to 6 hours | 4 to 5 |
| Eau de Parfum (EDP) | about 15 to 20% | roughly 5 to 8+ hours | 3 to 4 |
| Extrait de Parfum | about 20 to 40% | roughly 8 to 12+ hours | 2 |
| Parfum | about 20 to 40% | roughly 8 to 12+ hours | 2 |

The percentages are industry ranges, not a promise on the label. Houses keep the exact number to themselves.

**Eau de Toilette** is the lighter, brighter version. It opens loud, fades sooner, and that's part of the charm. Built for daytime, warm weather and spraying generously. [YSL La Nuit de L'Homme](/fragrance/yves-saint-laurent-la-nuit-de-l-homme/), the [Valentino Born in Roma](/collections/born-in-roma/) line and Margiela's [Jazz Club](/fragrance/maison-margiela-replica-jazz-club/) and Coffee Break are the EDTs in the collection.

**Eau de Parfum** is the sweet spot. Richer than an EDT, still easy to wear from the office to dinner. Most of the collection lives here: every [Tom Ford](/houses/tom-ford/), [Armani I Will](/fragrance/armani-i-will/), [Xerjoff Erba Pura](/fragrance/xerjoff-erba-pura/).

**Extrait de Parfum** is the most concentrated form a house makes. Denser, smoother, more expensive per ml, and it tends to hug the skin rather than fill the room. Every [Nishane](/houses/nishane/) is an extrait.

**Parfum** is the same top tier as Extrait, just the other name. When a bottle says only "Parfum" (not "Eau de"), it means the house's strongest, longest-lasting version. [Claude Marsal Or Noir Café](/fragrance/claude-marsal-or-noir-cafe/) and [La Mélodie Maximal](/fragrance/claude-marsal-la-melodie-maximal/) are the Parfums here.

## What concentration doesn't tell you

**Stronger isn't louder.** More oil mostly means longer wear and a deeper, rounder scent, not a bigger cloud. Plenty of extraits sit closer to the skin than a loud EDT. Projection is about the notes and the composition, not the tier.

**Same name, different juice.** An EDT and an EDP of the same fragrance are usually re-balanced recipes, not the same liquid watered down. That's why [Le Male Elixir](/fragrance/jean-paul-gaultier-le-male-elixir/), the Parfum, is its own scent and not the 1995 Eau de Toilette turned up.

**"Elixir", "Intense" and "X" aren't concentrations.** They're the house's names for a flanker or a stronger take. Look for the words Toilette, Parfum or Extrait to know the real tier.

## Longevity: what to actually expect

The ranges above assume skin, not clothes. Spray a scarf or a sweater and even an EDT lasts for days, but you skip the drydown, which is the real scent. On skin:

- An **EDT** gets you through a workday if you apply at 8 and don't mind it being quiet by 2. Reapply for the evening.
- An **EDP** gets you from the morning to dinner. Four sprays and you're set.
- An **Extrait** gets you through the day and into the next morning. Two sprays. You'll still catch it on your collar tomorrow.

Heat amplifies, cold mutes. The same EDP that fades by lunch in January will follow you around in July.

## How decant sizes map to days of wear

This is the part that matters when you're deciding between a 2, 5 or 10 ml. A fine-mist atomizer gives about 12 sprays per ml, so:

| Decant | Sprays | EDT (5 per wear) | EDP (3 to 4 per wear) | Extrait (2 per wear) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 ml | about 25 | 5 wears | 6 to 8 wears | about 12 wears |
| 3 ml Luxury | about 35 | 7 wears | 9 to 12 wears | about 17 wears |
| 5 ml | about 60 | 12 wears | 15 to 20 wears | about 30 wears |
| 10 ml | about 120 | 24 wears | 30 to 40 wears | about 60 wears |

Two things fall out of that table.

First, a **2 ml is a real test**, not a sniff. Even for an EDT you get five full wears, enough to try it on a workday, a night out and a lazy Sunday before you decide.

Second, **extraits are better value than they look.** A 5 ml Extrait at two sprays a day lasts about as long as a 10 ml EDT at five. When you compare the price per ml on a scent page, factor in the tier. A 10 ml of [Nishane Tempfluo](/fragrance/nishane-tempfluo/) is two months of daily wear.

## Picking a size

- **Trying something new:** 2 ml. Wear it three or four times in the setting you'd actually wear it.
- **You already know you like it:** 5 ml. Two to three weeks of regular wear, or a month of an extrait.
- **A signature, or a gift:** 10 ml, or a 10 ml Luxury bottle with the gold cap. A month or more of daily wear in a size that fits a jacket pocket.
- **Office and travel:** a 5 or 10 ml roll-on. No spray, no cloud, nothing to leak in a bag.

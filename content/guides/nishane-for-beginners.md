More of this shop comes from Nishane than from any other house, and there's a reason. Istanbul's first niche perfume house makes almost everything as an extrait de parfum, the strongest concentration there is, and builds every scent around a story. Two sprays last all day. The hard part is picking one. This is how I'd do it.

## First, what "extrait" means for you

Nishane bottles are extraits: roughly 20 to 40% fragrance oil, against 15 to 20% for a typical eau de parfum. In practice that means two sprays, not four, and a scent that's still on your skin the next morning. A 2 ml decant is about 25 sprays, which is a dozen wears. More on this in [EDT vs EDP vs Extrait](/guides/edt-vs-edp-vs-extrait/) and [Fragrance 101](/fragrance-101).

It also means a 10 ml decant goes a long way. Most people never need the 50 ml bottle.

## Hacivat: the one to try if you only try one

Pineapple, grapefruit and bergamot up front, then dry oakmoss and clean woods underneath. Bright, polished and confident, with a drydown that reads expensive. [Hacivat](/fragrance/nishane-hacivat/) is named for the composed, well-spoken character in Turkish shadow theatre, and it wears exactly like that.

**For:** anyone who wants to smell put-together. Sunny days, the office, spring and summer. It's the most-recommended Nishane for a reason. If you like fresh scents but find them forgettable, start here.

## Ani: the one people remember you by

Blue ginger and bergamot that warm into Turkish rose and blackcurrant, then vanilla, benzoin and sandalwood that stay close and warm for hours. [Ani](/fragrance/nishane-ani/) is a cult vanilla, and it earns it.

**For:** cold days and nights. Him, her, anyone. If you want one scent that works for a date and a Tuesday, this is it. If you don't like sweet at all, skip it.

## Fan Your Flames: the one for good company

Coconut and rum open sweet, tobacco smoke and tonka melt in, and cedar and oakmoss keep it from turning to syrup. [Fan Your Flames](/fragrance/nishane-fan-your-flames/) takes its name from Rumi and its scene from a night out with a coconut shisha.

**For:** late nights with your people once the snow is down. Warm, boozy and social. If that sounds like too much, [Fan Your Flames X](/fragrance/nishane-fan-your-flames-x/) is the same idea turned down: thyme and mastic make it greener and more tropical. [Compare the two](/compare/fyf-vs-fyf-x/).

## Wulong Cha: the one for hot days

Bergamot, orange and mandarin over a cup of oolong tea, then fig and clean musk. Nishane calls [Wulong Cha](/fragrance/nishane-wulong-cha/) one of the strongest fresh scents on the market, and it is. Calm and bright, like the first cup of a slow morning.

**For:** summer, mornings, anyone who wants fresh without citrus-cologne obvious. Its remix, [Wulong Cha X](/fragrance/nishane-wulong-cha-x/), swaps the oolong for green tea and magnolia and is even softer.

## The Time Capsule: five moods, one collection

Nishane handed each of these to a different master perfumer and gave each one a big idea. They share a bottle and nothing else. The [Time Capsule Collection](/collections/time-capsule/) bundles all five at 10% off, and the [comparison table](/compare/time-capsule/) puts them side by side.

- **[Tempfluo](/fragrance/nishane-tempfluo/)** · the flow of time. Mandarin and saffron melting into jasmine, orange flower and praline. Sweet, golden, brighter than the notes suggest. The easiest of the five to love, and a favourite for her.
- **[Kredo](/fragrance/nishane-kredo/)** · belief. Pink pepper and cardamom over osmanthus, then oud, sandalwood and soft suede. Calm, deep and sure of itself. For anyone who leans leather and oud but doesn't want loud.
- **[Papilefiko](/fragrance/nishane-papilefiko/)** · the butterfly effect. Cardamom and lavandin over fir balsam, moss and styrax, composed by Dominique Ropion. Crisp, aromatic, a little unexpected. For the person who doesn't want to smell like anyone else.
- **[Deziro](/fragrance/nishane-deziro/)** · desire. Mandarin and sea air over mint and star anise, then sandalwood and vetiver, by Alberto Morillas. Fresh and effortless. The warm-weather pick.
- **[Tero](/fragrance/nishane-tero/)** · earth. Salted caramel against two peppers, then patchouli, cinnamon bark, vetiver and oak. Rich, earthy and quietly commanding. The cold-weather pick.

## Where to start

If you want a simple answer: **Hacivat** for day, **Ani** for night. Try both as 2 ml decants and you'll know within a week which way you lean.

If you already know your taste:

| You like | Try first | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh and clean | Hacivat | Wulong Cha X, Deziro |
| Sweet and warm | Ani | Tempfluo, Fan Your Flames |
| Leather, oud, suede | Kredo | Suède et Safran |
| Earthy and spicy | Tero | Papilefiko |
| Boozy and smoky | Fan Your Flames | Jazz Club |

Every Nishane here is poured from a sealed full bottle, in 2, 5 or 10 ml, with Ottawa pickup or shipping across Canada. See [everything from the house](/houses/nishane/), or let the [Scent Sherpa](/#quiz) narrow it down.
